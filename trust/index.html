Trust Center

Everything an administrator should ask before installing Resultary.

A concise view of architecture, permissions, data handling and operational boundaries for the beta.

Platform

Atlassian Forge for Jira Cloud, with Forge-hosted persistent app storage.

Automation access

Resultary does not modify, enable, disable or delete Jira Automation rules.

Personal data

Jira account IDs may be stored for incident assignment or escalation and are handled through Forge privacy reporting.

External egress

Optional Slack / Teams notification endpoints configured by an administrator.

Data residency

Forge-hosted data follows supported Atlassian data-residency behavior; optional external notifications follow the chosen destination service.

Security contact

support@getresultary.com

Important product boundaries

Resultary verifies configured evidence

It cannot prove business success outside the evidence and timing rules an administrator chooses.

Resultary is not a loss guarantee

Value metrics are estimates based on administrator-provided values and verified recovery, not a promise of realized financial savings.
