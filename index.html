THE PROBLEM

A green checkmark
isn’t the whole story.

Automations are built to achieve something: update a record, complete a handoff, move work forward. Resultary brings attention to the business outcome, not just the technical execution.

HOW IT WORKS

From “it ran” to “it worked.”

Three clear steps to turn uncertainty into an actionable signal.

01↗

Observe the run

Know whether your protected automation sent the signal you expected.

02◎

Verify the result

Check the evidence that represents success, instead of trusting a run status alone.

03✓

Close the loop

Make persistent failures visible, assign ownership and confirm recovery.

These capabilities are available in the current Jira edition. Other integrations are in development or planned.
